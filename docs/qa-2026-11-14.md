# Alea: what's missing, and what testing found (2026-11-14)

A hands-on pass through the app on a local test copy: a brand-new account from sign-up to its first series, then a host account with live bets. Desktop width (1280px) and phone width (390px). The test copy uses the local Supabase stand-in (`tests/sql/local-supabase.mjs`), so no test accounts were made on the real project.

## Do these first

1. **Phones: the full menu sits above every page.** The sidebar takes the first ~560px of every screen. On a bet page the wager form starts about 1,200px down, more than two screens of scrolling before you can bet. Most people will use this on their phones.
2. **Apply migration `202611140016_series_from_clan.sql` to the real Supabase project.** Until then, "Bet together" from a clan creates the series but fails when it adds the clan's members.
3. **New pages open mid-scroll.** Moving to another page keeps the old scroll position. From Home scrolled to 500px, tapping a bet opened the bet page already scrolled 438px down, past the title and the odds.

## What's missing

| Gap | Where it shows up | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Walkthrough on first sign-in | New accounts land on Home | The Tutorial exists, but nothing opens it automatically and there is no on-screen tour. |
| Compact phone navigation | Every page at phone width | See "Do these first" #1. |
| Forgot password | Sign-in screen | There's no reset link, and no way to change your email or password. |
| Google sign-in | Sign-in screen | The button is disabled. It needs the Google provider turned on in Supabase, and the redirect URL `http://localhost:3000/**` (plus the production URL) added. |
| Notifications | Whole app | Nothing tells you when a bet opens, a result lands or it's your turn, unless the app is open. The only signal is the Home badge. |
| Friends from people you play with | Friends page | A player in a 3-person clan, with 2 series against the same people, still sees "No friends yet". Offer "People you've played with" as one-tap adds. |
| Alea Wrapped is hard to find | Profile only | It isn't in the sidebar and the Tutorial doesn't mention it. |
| Untested on the real project | Profile photo upload, email confirmation | The stand-in has no file storage, and it doesn't send confirmation emails. Check both against the real project once. |
| Server names out of date | `src/app/api/series/[seriesId]/favors/...` | The UI says "punishments", but the API routes and SQL still say "favors". |

## What worked well

- **Sign-up:** sign-up and sign-in work, and a wrong password shows a clear message.
- **Join with a code:** it takes lowercase codes and full invite links. Wrong codes and started series get plain-English errors.
- **Lobby:** "Ready" is blocked until you've added a punishment, and the message says so. Adding a punishment resets everyone's ready, and the activity feed explains why.
- **Tutorial:** the first-bet checklist ticks off from the account's real progress (3 of 5 for the new player, "Done!" for the host).
- **Practice bet:** the payout table matches the server's rules, including the leftover coin going to the biggest stake.
- **Betting:** wagers, add-to-wager and the withdrawal countdown work. The host's resolve takes two taps, and the payouts table and leaderboard update straight away.
- **The House** commentary shows up on bets and results, and can be dismissed or turned off.
- **Console:** no app errors on any page. The only red lines were the expected 409 and 400 responses from the wrong-code and wrong-password tests, plus dev-server reload noise.

## Quality-of-life ideas

- **Start where you can bet.** On a bet page at phone width, put the wager form first, or keep a sticky "Bet" bar at the bottom.
- **Say why a wager is blocked.** Show "You have 1,020 free" under the amount when it's too high, and add a "Max" chip next to "All".
- **Clearer Your moves rows.** Series names are cut to "PLACEHOLDER C…", which can't tell apart two series that start with the same word. Put the series name on its own line.
- **Fewer repeated buttons for new players.** A new player's Home shows "Start a bet" and "Join with a code" twice, in the action cards and in the empty Your bets panel, and Your moves says it a third time. Keep the cards and make the empty states point at the Tutorial.
- **Inline lobby checks.** Disable the Ready box, with a hint, until you've added a punishment, rather than letting the click fail.
- **Coins wording.** Use "Coins" and "Free to bet" consistently across the series header, the bet page and the leaderboard.

## How this was tested

- **Accounts:** a new account signed up through the UI, plus three seeded players (Ana, Ben, Cat). The seeded players share a clan, a live series with four bets, and a second series in its lobby.
- **Flows covered:** sign-up, sign-in and sign-out, joining with good and bad codes, the lobby (punishments and ready), the Tutorial (checklist and practice bet), Home, wager limits, placing a wager, resolving a bet, payouts, the leaderboard, Friends, Clans, Profile and phone width.
- **Not covered:** clan chat, multiple-choice and range bets, the end of a series (punishment picks and paying up) and Wrapped. Earlier automated runs cover these: chat 9/9, multiple choice 9/9, ranges 8/8, SQL 91/91.
